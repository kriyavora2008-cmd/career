Top 6 Algorithmic Patterns to Master

1. Two Pointers: Ideal for sorted arrays/strings, searching pairs, palindrome checks, and container area problems.

2. Sliding Window: For finding sub-arrays/sub-strings of size k or matching constraints (longest substring without repeating chars).

3. Monotonic Stack: For next greater/smaller element queries, histogram areas, and daily temperature comparisons.

4. Fast and Slow Pointers (Floyd's Tortoise & Hare): Cycle detection in linked lists and middle node extraction.

5. Breadth-First Search (BFS) vs DFS: BFS guarantees shortest path in unweighted graphs; DFS is preferred for backtracking, tree traversals, and topological sorting.

6. Dynamic Programming: Identifying optimal substructure and overlapping subproblems. Recognize whether problem is 0/1 Knapsack, Longest Common Subsequence, or Grid DP.
